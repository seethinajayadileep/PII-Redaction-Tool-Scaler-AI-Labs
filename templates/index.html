What gets replaced

  • Person names
  • Emails
  • Phone numbers
  • Company / trust names
  • Addresses
  • SSN, credit cards, DOB, IP

Left unchanged

CIN, PAN, DIN, rupee amounts, share counts, page numbers, and statute names are treated as document data, not personal data.

How names are found

Structured types (email, phone, SSN, card, IP, DOB) use patterns. People and companies also use a known list for this prospectus, plus lines like Contact Person: and cue words such as from / Dear. A brand-new name with no cue can still be missed.
